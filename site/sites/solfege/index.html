Straight from the music classroom

Every note has its own color

The answer buttons look just like Boomwhackers and handbells, so kids who play them in class feel right at home, and everyone else quickly links each color to a note. Reading music becomes something they can see, tap and feel.

1

Spot the note

A friendly highlight moves along the staff, giving every note the same amount of time.

2

Tap the tube

Each tube presses down like a toy. Get it right and the note lights up in its color.

3

Learn from mistakes

Miss one and the right name appears under the note, so every round teaches something.

Practice that fits

Start simple, grow at their pace

Pick exactly what your child is working on in lessons, and Solfege builds games from it.

  • Treble and bass clef
  • All 15 major keys, with real key signatures
  • Any range of notes, from a handful to the whole staff
  • Whole notes to sixteenths, at the speed you choose

Level up

The chord game

Ready for a challenge? Read three notes at once and name every one of them. It's the perfect next step for kids starting to play with both hands.

Celebrate every win

Stars, streaks and “Superstar!”

Kids earn a star for every right note and build streaks as they go. At the end of each one-minute game they see how they did, with honest, encouraging titles that keep them coming back to beat their best.

For the whole family

See how they're really doing

Every child gets their own profile, avatar and settings. The progress report shows how often they practice, their best notes per minute, the notes they've mastered and the ones that need more work, so you know exactly what to focus on next.
