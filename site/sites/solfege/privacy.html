Solfege Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

In short: Solfege keeps your children's profiles and practice history on your device. We don't collect names, photos, email addresses, locations or anything else that identifies your child or family. There are no ads, and we never sell or share data. To make Solfege better, the app sends us anonymous usage data (like which screens are visited and how long a game lasts) and, if it crashes, an anonymous crash report.

Who we are

Solfege is an app by Bleext LLC ("we") that helps children learn to read music. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it's kept and who can see it. If you have a question, email us at contact@bleext.com.

Information kept on your device

To work, Solfege saves the following on your iPhone or iPad. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

  • Profiles: the name you type for each child and the avatar you choose. If you choose a photo as an avatar, the app keeps a copy of that one photo. The app can only see the photo you pick, not the rest of your photo library.
  • Practice history: the notes each child answered, whether they were right, how long they took, and their game scores. This is used for the progress report in the app.
  • Settings: each child's chosen clef, key, game type and similar options.

Like other app data, this information may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by your Apple Account settings, not by us.

Anonymous usage data

To learn which parts of Solfege children enjoy and where the app can be improved, it sends anonymous usage data to our own server. We don't use a third-party analytics company. Usage data includes:

  • which screens are opened and which buttons are tapped, for example "Let's play!", "Play again" or the membership button;
  • game details: the game type, clef and key, how long a game lasted, how many answers were right, wrong or missed, and the best streak;
  • which settings are changed and their new value (for example, the clef or how many notes are turned on, but not which notes);
  • steps in the membership screen, such as whether it was opened, closed, or a purchase was started, finished or cancelled;
  • whether a profile's avatar is one of our animals or a photo (never the photo itself or which animal);
  • a random number created when the app is installed, and a session number that changes each time the app is opened. These let us count how many people use a feature without knowing who they are. The random number isn't linked to your child, your device, your Apple Account or any advertising identifier, and it's erased when you delete the app.

Usage data never includes your child's name, photos, the notes they practiced, your email address, your device's name or any advertising identifier. Like every internet connection, the app's connection to our server reveals your IP address. Our server uses it once to look up an approximate region (such as a country and state) and then discards it. It never stores your IP address. We keep usage data for about six months and then delete it automatically. It is only used to improve Solfege. We don't sell it, share it, use it for advertising or use it to build a profile of anyone.

Crash reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a service we use to find and fix bugs. A crash report contains:

  • technical details about the error, such as the part of the app's code where it happened;
  • the device model (for example, "iPhone 16"), iOS version, available memory and the app's version.

Crash reports do not contain your child's name, photos, practice history, your email address, your IP address, your location, your language or time zone, or any advertising identifier. We set up the app and our Sentry account to remove or block this information. Sentry keeps crash reports for a limited time and then deletes them automatically.

Purchases

Membership is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple only tells the app whether a membership is active. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases and links that leave the app are protected by a grown-up check, so a child can't start a purchase or open a website without an adult.

What we don't do

  • We don't create accounts or ask for an email address.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't use the advertising identifier.
  • We don't sell or share information with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't use your device's location (GPS), camera or microphone.

Children's privacy

Solfege is made for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, including under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Everything personal stays on your device. The only information that leaves it is the anonymous usage data and crash reports described above. They contain nothing that identifies a child or family, and we use them only to keep Solfege working and to make it better.

Removing your information

Deleting a profile in Solfege hides it from the app, but its name, photo and practice history stay on the device. To remove all of Solfege's information, delete the app from your device. This deletes everything the app has stored, including the random number used for usage data. Because we don't have your family's information, there is nothing on our side that we could link to you. Anonymous usage data and crash reports are deleted automatically after a limited time. If you have questions about either, email us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Solfege handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change means more information would leave your device, we'll update the app's privacy details in the App Store before it happens.
